A growing, sourced collection of publicly documented stories about how non-intoxicating CBD, CBG, and other legal cannabinoids fit into real lives — gathered to put human faces on the 2026 hemp-law debate. Every entry links to its original public source.

🌿 How this library works (our promise)

  • Only public, attributable sources. Every story below is drawn from published news reporting, official records, or on-the-record advocacy — each with a citation you can verify. We do not scrape or repost private social-media posts.
  • Real & representative. Following FTC guidance on testimonials, we present genuine accounts and note that individual results are not typical or guaranteed.
  • Stories, not medical claims. These are lived experiences, not evidence that any product treats disease. See the science, with citations, on our Know the Facts page.
  • Consent-first for first-party stories. Community stories are published only with explicit permission, and you may remain anonymous.

Anxiety / StressCBG
Peer-reviewed · Scientific Reports
"20 mg of hemp-derived CBG significantly reduced anxiety — without intoxication."
WSU clinical trial (34 participants)

The first human clinical trial of CBG found measurable reductions in anxiety and stress vs. placebo, with no impairment and even improved verbal memory — early but rigorous evidence for a non-intoxicating cannabinoid.

Source: Cuttler et al., Scientific Reports (2024). Read →
